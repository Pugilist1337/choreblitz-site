ChoreBlitz

ChoreBlitz helps households share the housework fairly. Short, timed cleaning Blitzes get chores done, everyone sees the real split of who does what — including the invisible to-dos — and appreciation goes to the people doing the work.

Subscription

One subscription covers your whole household. Payments are handled by Apple. To cancel or change your plan, open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting your ChoreBlitz account does not cancel the subscription.

Got a new phone? Tap Restore purchases on the ChoreBlitz subscription screen to get your access back.

Delete your account

In the app, open Settings and tap Delete account. This deletes your account and your data. If you are the only adult in your household, the household is deleted too.
